Detective

A mobile pass-and-play word bluffing game.

Players secretly receive words. One player is the impostor. The group gives clues, discusses, and votes who to eliminate.

How to Play

Civilians all receive the same word.

The impostor receives a different but related word.

If there are 5 or fewer players, the impostor also receives a small clue about the civilian word.

If there are 6 or more players, the impostor receives no clue.

After the timer starts, players give clues and discuss. When time ends, or when players vote early, the group chooses who they think is the impostor.

Game Setup

Minimum players: 3. This version uses exactly 1 impostor per round.
